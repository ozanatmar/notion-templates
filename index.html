Notion Template Helpers

Widgets meant to be embedded in a Notion page with /embed. No accounts, no tracking, no API keys. Everything is computed in your browser; live prices come from this site's own cached endpoint.

Crypto / FX Trading Dashboard v2

  • Trade Analytics — Crypto / FX Trading Dashboard v2
    Equity curve, R-multiple by trade, P&L by setup, and a closed-trades table with KPI tiles. Paste your own trade log or explore the sample data.
    /template-helpers/Crypto-FX-Trading-Dashboard-v2-trade-analytics/
  • Cross-Exchange Spread — Crypto / FX Trading Dashboard v2
    Live board showing the same asset priced across Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken and Coinbase at once, with the cheapest and richest venue and the spread between them.
    /template-helpers/Crypto-FX-Trading-Dashboard-v2-cross-exchange-spread/
  • Live Chart — Crypto / FX Trading Dashboard v2
    Full TradingView advanced chart, dark themed and sized to the embed. Set the pair with ?symbol=, or switch it inside the chart.
    /template-helpers/Crypto-FX-Trading-Dashboard-v2-live-chart/
  • Live Ticker — Crypto / FX Trading Dashboard v2
    Continuously scrolling strip of 30 major crypto pairs with live price and 24h change, each with its coin icon. Sits across the top of a page.
    /template-helpers/Crypto-FX-Trading-Dashboard-v2-ticker-tape/
  • Economic Calendar — Crypto / FX Trading Dashboard v2
    High-importance releases for the major currencies, from TradingView. Set the currencies with ?currencies=.
    /template-helpers/Crypto-FX-Trading-Dashboard-v2-economic-calendar/

Rental Property Deal Analyzer

  • Deal Analyzer — Rental Property Deal Analyzer
    Underwrite a rental in one screen: levered IRR, cash-on-cash, a rent-vs-price sensitivity heatmap and a full pro forma. Paste a Deal export cell or type the numbers.
    /template-helpers/Rental-Property-Deal-Analyzer-analyzer/

How to embed. Open a widget, copy its URL, then in Notion type /embed, paste the URL and drag the block to the height you want.
Privacy. Widgets compute in your browser. Data you paste stays in the browser tab and is never sent anywhere. The spread board reads public exchange prices through this site's own cached endpoint — it sends nothing about you.
