Mid = (best bid + best ask) / 2. low is the cheapest venue, high the richest.

Mid price per exchange for each asset, with the cheapest and richest venue tagged and the spread between them.

Spot mid prices, public exchange APIs, cached ~10s. Prices are indicative, not executable quotes; a venue that fails to answer is shown as “—” and left out of that row’s spread.
